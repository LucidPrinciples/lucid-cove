Frequently Asked Questions

Why are the Echoes tuned to 432Hz instead of standard 440Hz?

Every Echo is tuned to A=432Hz instead of the modern standard A=440Hz. The 8Hz difference shifts the entire harmonic spectrum of the music. Studies have shown 432Hz tuning is associated with reduced heart rate and blood pressure compared to 440Hz, and listeners consistently report it feeling more natural and less fatiguing. For a system designed to entrain your nervous system, we chose the tuning that works with your body's natural resonance, not against it.
